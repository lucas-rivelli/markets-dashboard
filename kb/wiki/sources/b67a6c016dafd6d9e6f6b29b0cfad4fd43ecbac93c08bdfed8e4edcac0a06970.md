## Key Claims
- By: Nicholas Grous and Varshika Prasanna The promise of AI wearables is perfect information recall paired with an AI agent, representing something substantially different from today’s assistants.
- Instead of asking AI generic questions, you could interact with an AI agent that knows the surrounding context about you – like history, patterns, and commitments.
- It could help organize your tasks, remind you of what was said in a meeting, surface commitments you forgot, or help reflect on your behaviors.
- The value is not just the information, but organized memory and contextual awareness applied to your own life.

## Evidence
- AI wearables are a bet that if AI could see and hear what you do and remember it for you, it would become exponentially more valuable.
- The question is not whether AI wearable technology will work. It is whether we are comfortable with a world in which memory is no longer scarce, and forgetting is no longer the default.
