## Key Claims
- The more time I spend helping people learn how to use AI, the more convinced I am that success with AI is less about technical ability and more about mindset.
- To borrow from Ralph Waldo Emerson, a line I have often used with my children about life, AI is a succession of lessons that must be lived to be understood.
- You do not really learn it by reading instructions.
- You learn it by running into roadblocks, adjusting, and trying again.
- The people who are moving fastest are often the people with little knowledge of how a computer works.
## Evidence
- Because AI is a probability machine. To use a probability machine well, you need a probability mindset.
- AI changes that. The cost of going in the wrong direction has collapsed.
